"ABOUT YOU AWARDS 2021"

In 2021 I was nominated with 2 more contestants for the "ABOUT YOU AWARDS 2021". This established award honors outstanding personalities in the creative industry from all sectors, such as fashion, music, sports, business, digital art and so on. I was nominated in the category style and could stand out with my very creative and unusual content visualizations. Unfortunately, I did not win the award, but was very pleased to be one of the most standing out creatives in 2021.
