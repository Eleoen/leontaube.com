PHOTOGRAPHY

Photography is one of the first creative disciplines I've learned in my life. And it's still my favorite today. I loved recording things with my dad's old camcorder and digital camera when I was a kid. When I was 13 I got my first DSLR resulting in hours of learning and improving my skills by doing photography whenever I could. Throughout my study I improved my skills even more by learning from professional photographers from all over the world. Especially in my semester abroad I've learned a lot from a well-known fashion photographer based in Thailand. I have experience in tons of different photography issues - business, product, wedding, fashion, nature, portraits, sports... The list goes on.
