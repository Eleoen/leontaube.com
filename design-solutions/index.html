DESIGN PROJECTS

Throughout my study I had the chance to work with several businesses on real cases. Using different design methods we managed to create concepts for possible design solutions for given problems. Nonetheless, I've also done multiple freelance jobs.

LOGO DESIGN "TRUE11"
This was my first paid logo design for a real client. True11 is an upcoming brand which is reworking vintage Porsche seat fabrics into clothing. Our goal was to create a connection between the sportscar brand Porsche and True11. So, I tried to implement different Porsche features in the logo design. In the last two pictures you can see the final results. The colored blocks visualize Porsche's iconic rear lights from the 90's.

UBIRCH EXHIBITION BOOTH
We partnered with UBIRCH to design an exhibition booth to increase the amount of visitors on the one hand and to improve the customer satisfaction on the other hand.
UBIRCH is focusing on IoT solutions and stands for security and technology. Since the target audience on technology exhibitions is male dominated we decided to go for a gamification solution with remote controlled cars.
Visitors get invited to the booth by email and can securely submit their registration to join the competition. At the booth the contestants have to master a course with the rc car on time. The best times are rewarded with different prizes like electronic devices or a partnership with UBIRCH.

SMART CITY BOCHOLT
This was a cooperation with the city marketing of Bocholt. Goal of this initiative project is the digitalization and implementation of smart features for the city of Bocholt. The smart city expert Frank Tentler operated as a connector between the city marketing and our university and helped us in every stage of the process. In this project we worked in small groups on different topics but tried to give Bocholt an all-round solution.
Our team had the task to be the supervision. We thought about events and marketing to gain attention for the project. We also worked on a solution for visitors to get introduced in the new smart city concept and how they can use it. Furthermore, we created a booklet with all the knowledge and concepts we came along the process to hand out the city marketing team.
At the end of the project we presented our results in front of the city marketing team.
Jürgen Block, managing director of the "Bundesvereinigung City- und Stadtmarketing Deutschland e.V" said about this project: "I have not seen such a range of innovative concepts from students yet".

2019 DESIGN EXHIBITION
In cooperation with the Cologne Design Conference (CDC) our degree was invited to plan an exhibition in context of design and sustainability. Everyone had different ideas what they wanted to show but in the end we've managed to transform a whole room into one big experienceable exhibition.
My idea was to make something interactive and I instantly thought of a photo booth - because this is something you always want to try out if you see one. I then connected the idea with a relevant topic at this time which was Greta Thunberg and her Fridays for Future movement. People could take a photo at the booth to remember that day and maybe even share it on social media to generate attention for the topic.
