SOCIAL MEDIA

I'm running a successful personal Instagram account since 2018. I know the little tricks of several platforms and am always learning about algorithm changes and news in the niche. Recently, I've even grown multiple TikTok accounts with ease. The main goal of my page is to make people stick to my content. If I should describe my content in 3 words they would be: surreal, creative and limitless. I've done a lot of cooperations with well known brands such as "ABOUT YOU", "Weekday", "Pinqponq", "ASICS", "Footlocker" and many more. So I have experience in directing my own creative work and matching it with the company's guidelines. At the same time, I also like to share my personal work, where I can live out my whole creativity without any limitations. Furthermore, I managed several social media channels for clients like Officina del Gusto, Radius Design and Radius Garage.
