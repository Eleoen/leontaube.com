ENTREPRENEURSHIP

In 2020 I started a small business next to my studies. I've always loved fashion and second-hand clothing, so I sold vintage pieces through my own little online shop called "NINE TEES Vintage". On this journey I learned a lot about entrepreneurship and what it means to be independent. I was a one man army and did everything myself: design, marketing, distribution, warehouse, accounting and customer support. A lot to juggle, but it taught me something about every single part of running a business. The shop is closed by now. Below you can see some of the work I did for it.

WEB DESIGN
I built the webshop on Shopify and customized it to fit my needs, with several apps to improve the performance. It was optimized for mobile devices too.
CORPORATE IDENTITY KIT
I designed a whole corporate identity including physical items such as business cards, hangtags, envelopes, stickers, goodies and packaging.
PRODUCT PHOTOGRAPHY
I shot every single piece of clothing for the webshop and masked it in Photoshop.
Here you can see the rework collection, for example: damaged or stained clothing was repaired or turned into new products. A creative and sustainable way to deal with low-quality pieces in the deliveries.
